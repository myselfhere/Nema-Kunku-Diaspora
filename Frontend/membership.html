Become a Member

Nema Kunku Diaspora welcomes all who believe in our mission to join and contribute to meaningful community development in The Gambia.

Who Can Join?

Membership is open to any individual committed to unity, transparency, and the sustainable development of Nema Kunku and its people—both at home and in the diaspora.

Benefits of Membership

  • Participate in meetings and decision-making
  • Receive updates on community projects and finances
  • Be eligible for support during emergencies
  • Contribute directly to lasting change in Nema Kunku

Contribution Options

Members contribute using one of the following plans:

  • €100 annually (January–December)
  • €50 semi-annually (paid twice a year)

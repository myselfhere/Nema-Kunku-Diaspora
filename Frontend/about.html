About Nema Kunku Diaspora

We are a community-focused association uniting our people since 2018.

Our Mission

To unite members of Nema Kunku at home and abroad to support development, solidarity, and sustainable impact through transparent projects and good governance.

Our Vision

A stronger, self-reliant community where every member contributes and benefits.

Core Values

  • Unity & Solidarity
  • Transparency & Accountability
  • Service & Volunteerism
  • Respect & Inclusion

Meetings

General meetings are held on the last Sunday of each month at 16:00 CET. Emergency meetings can be called with at least 7 days’ notice.
